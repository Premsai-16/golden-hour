How it works

1
SOS pressed

The phone captures type and GPS location and sends it over a WebSocket.

2
Nearest suitable hospital

The server checks beds or blood stock and picks the closest hospital that can help.

3
Volunteers within 3 km

The Haversine formula finds nearby verified volunteers and pushes the alert.

4
Help is on the way

Everyone sees live status: hospital ready, volunteer coming.
